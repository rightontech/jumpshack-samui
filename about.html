The Story

Born Above Chaweng

Koh Samui had everything — except a view from above it all. We fixed that.

How It Started

The Island Had the Beaches. We Found the Mountain.

Koh Samui is famous for its beaches. Long stretches of white sand, beach clubs at sea level, the same flat horizon you've seen a hundred times before. Beautiful? Absolutely. But we kept thinking: what if you could get above all of it?

Tucked into the hillside above Chaweng, the Jump Shack site had been sitting there waiting. From this vantage point, you can see Chaweng Lake to the south, the full arc of the beach below, and the green mountain peaks stretching north. No beach in Samui gives you this perspective.

We opened in December 2025 with one simple idea: build a place that feels like a premium beach club, but perched where only a mountain can put you. Add proper diving boards — the kind that make your heart beat faster — and you have something the island has never had before.

Jump Shack isn't trying to compete with the beach clubs on the sand. We're somewhere different entirely. Come up once and you'll understand.

Milestones

How We Got Here

2024 — The Idea

Finding the Mountain

The site above Chaweng was identified. The view confirmed everything. Planning began for Koh Samui's first mountain beach club.

Early 2025 — Build

Construction Begins

The pool, the boards, the bar, the sunbed terraces — carved into the hillside with the view front-of-mind at every decision point.

December 2025 — Launch

Jump Shack Opens

The gates opened. The 5m board got its first jumpers. The cocktails were poured. Samui had something new above Chaweng.

2026 — Growing

Word Spreads

Pool parties, Sunday Roasts, private events, TikTok moments. Jump Shack became the answer to "what should we do in Samui?"
