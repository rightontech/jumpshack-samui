FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Jump Shack is located above Chaweng on Koh Samui. The easiest way to reach us is by scooter, taxi, or Grab. WhatsApp us for the exact directions link — the GPS coordinates can vary between maps apps, so we recommend getting the pin directly from us before heading up.

Walk-ins are welcome most days. However, we recommend booking ahead for Saturday Pool Parties, Sunday Roast, VIP tables, and group visits of 6+. WhatsApp us on +66 855 758 510 to reserve.

The 3m board is open to guests who are confident swimmers. The 5m board is recommended for adults and older teenagers (16+). Children under 16 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian. Our lifeguard is on duty throughout opening hours and will advise on the day.

Smart casual. Swimwear is welcome poolside. We ask that you cover up for entry and when in the bar and dining areas. Flip flops and sandals are fine. Smart shorts, sundresses, and light summer clothing all work perfectly. No football shirts or sleeveless vests at the bar please.

We operate through light rain — the view is still spectacular and the bar stays open. During heavy storms, the diving boards may be temporarily closed for safety. We will update our Instagram and WhatsApp if we need to close unexpectedly due to weather. Koh Samui's dry season (Dec–April) is the best time to visit.

Yes — Jump Shack is available for exclusive private hire. Birthdays, corporate events, brand activations, and weddings are all possible. We work with you to create a bespoke package including catering, entertainment, and decoration. Send us a message or WhatsApp us with your date and group size to discuss.
