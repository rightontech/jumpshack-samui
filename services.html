The Jump

Samui's Most Photographed Spot

The 3m and 5m diving boards at Jump Shack are unlike any you've seen. You're not just jumping into a pool — you're launching off a mountain with the whole island spread out below you.

The 5m board is the ultimate Samui photo. The 3m is your warm-up. Both are open throughout the day for all guests.

Open to all entry guests — no extra charge

Relax

Sunbeds & The Pool

Settle into a sunbed, order a cocktail, and do absolutely nothing — while looking at arguably the best view on Koh Samui. The pool is clean, the sun is always perfect, and the crowd is never too big.

Standard entry includes sunbed access. VIP tables come with dedicated table service and priority sunbed reservation.
